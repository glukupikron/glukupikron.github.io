Am I
   in Love?
—


Yes,




        since I’m







                      waiting.


Discover your paper to write on.

While you wait, you’ve spotted a Sacred Cat named Neko. what would you offer?

Would you like to wait for the hanabi to start again?
While you wait, you’ve spotted a lonely jukebox named Ta-da. You can insert a coin to play a track you want to be played.

Has the one you have been waiting for arrived?
You can take away your record of waiting.
